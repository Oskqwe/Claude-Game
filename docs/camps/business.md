# Camp 3: The Man of Business

**Anchor:** Neville Chamberlain. **Divergence:** drastic (this camp owns *The Peace of 1940*). **Vector:** order +7, empire +4, state −2, steel −8, loyalty +6, temper −7.

The hardest camp to write fairly. The player must feel why reasonable, hard-working, patriotic men chose appeasement, and the dice must let it succeed or fail on its merits.

Notes:

- **Wilson route** is the only route in any camp where the player may never sit in the Commons. Treat it as a mandarin-like life whose political power runs through the PM. It needs a flag on Act III scenes so that "your" voice is advice, not votes. Borrow mechanics from the Mandarin camp.
- **Halifax** is not a route (an aristocrat's life does not fit a businessman's camp), but he is the key relationship for the whole camp.

## Act II hinges (1918–1938)

- **1917, Director of National Service** (spine): resign, or stay and be humiliated by the Prime Minister. Sets the lifelong `@gwynne` feud.
- **1924–29, Minister of Health** (spine): 21 of 25 bills; the Poor Law guardians abolished. A QT set.
- **1931, the Liberal split** (Simon route): lead the Liberal Nationals into the National Government.
- **Dec 1935, Hoare–Laval** (Hoare route, any route in the Cabinet): stand by the Foreign Secretary or sacrifice him.
- **Oct 1936, Cable Street** (Simon route as Home Secretary): ban the march or not. Hard subject.
- **1938, Air Secretary** (Wood): shadow factories, and the choice of fighters over bombers.

## Act III: this camp's angle

- **Berchtesgaden, Godesberg, Munich.** The player flies. Hallifield's Cabinet turn against Godesberg is a hinge.
- **26 Sept 1938.** Send Wilton with the letter, or go again yourself.
- **March 1939.** The "golden age" speech days before Prague (Hoad).
- **31 March 1939.** The Polish guarantee, driven by Hallifield.
- **July 1939.** Wilton and Wohlthat: an economic deal for Germany. A path into *The Peace of 1940*.
- **2 September 1939.** Simmons leads the ministers' deputation. The player, as PM, faces it.
- **May 1940.** Norway; then, if still PM or still in the War Cabinet, the 25–28 May argument. This camp may choose Hallifield's side, and the spine's *Peace of 1940* opens.
- **Death.** Chamberlain died of cancer in November 1940. The player's health strand should make this a risk, not a certainty.
- **If the player survives** (the long divergence): 1941–51 as an elder statesman, the peace's custodian, or a disgraced backbencher.
