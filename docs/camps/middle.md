# Camp 6: The Middle Way

**Anchor:** Harold Macmillan. **Divergence:** mild. **Vector:** order +2, empire +1, state +4, steel +6, loyalty −2, temper +1.

Young Tories who rebelled over appeasement and wanted a planned, humane capitalism. The camp's Act III is about when to break with your own side and how to come back.

## Act II hinges (1924–1938)

- **1926–29, Stockton:** the unemployed town and the "YMCA" group of young Tories (GK name).
- **1929, defeat;** 1930, the marriage strand: the wife and the friend (Boothroyd). A private hard choice: confront, ignore, divorce. Divorce ends a Tory career in the period.
- **1932, Hitler** (Boothroyd): meet him and warn.
- **1935, India Act** (Buller): carry it against the Bulldog.
- **1936, Abyssinia:** resign the whip.
- **1938, *The Middle Way*** (GK title and year: 1938). Planning for Tories.
- **Feb 1938, Edgerton resigns** over Italy. Follow, or stay.
- **Oct 1938, the Oxford by-election** (Hoggart): stand as the Munich man, or campaign for the anti-Munich candidate.
