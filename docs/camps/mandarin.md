# Camp 10: The Mandarin

**Anchor:** John Anderson. **Divergence:** mild, behind the scenes. **Vector:** order +3, empire +1, state +2, steel +2, loyalty +10, temper −10.

Officials who ran the country while others made speeches. Most of this camp never sits in the Commons, so it needs its own mechanics: `cap` becomes influence with ministers; `seat` is replaced by **standing in Whitehall**; and the player's choices are memos, minutes and quiet words, not votes.

## Act II hinges (1920–1938)

- **1920–22, Dublin Castle:** reprisals, the truce, the Treaty. Hard subject.

- **1938, the Commons** (spine): Scottish Universities, as an Independent.

## Mechanics this camp needs

- **Minutes.** A "minute" doc type (small, typed, initialled) beside letters and telegrams.
- **No seat.** The wilderness rule (losing your seat) does not apply. Replace with "moved sideways".
- **Influence.** Relationships with ministers matter more than the press. `press` should be near-invisible.

## Set pieces

- **Lunch:** at the Athenaeum with Brydon and Cadell, 1939 (new place: club, could adapt `carlton` with a different palette).
- **QT set:** none as an official. Instead, **briefing the minister before Questions**: five supplementaries, the player writes the notes. Same `qt` mechanics.
