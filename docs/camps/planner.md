# Camp 7: The Planner

**Anchor:** Hugh Dalton. **Divergence:** moderate. **Vector:** order −4, empire −3, state +10, steel +4, loyalty +3, temper −2.

Clever socialists who believed the state could be run like a well-designed machine, and found out how hard the Treasury is. The camp's currency is numbers: give it a "the pound" meter in Act III.

## Act II hinges (1924–1938)

- **1924–31, the economist MP:** Peckham; Bishop Auckland.
- **1931, MacDonald's defection:** follow him or stay.
- **1932–37, the XYZ Club and the New Fabian Research Bureau** (GK names): build the plans.
- **1937, *Labour's Immediate Programme*** (GK); rearmament against the left.
- **Socialist League** (Crisp): the Unity Campaign and the threat of expulsion.
- **1934, Vienna** (Gaskell route): watch the socialists crushed.

## Act III: this camp's angle

- **1939, expulsion** (Crisp).
- **1940–42, Economic Warfare:** set up SOE; the "ministry of ungentlemanly warfare" (GK phrase). Feuds with Wavell and Duff Cowper.
- **1942, Moscow and India** (Crisp): the India mission's failure. Hard subject.
- **1942, coal rationing** dropped after a Tory revolt.
- **1945, the Treasury:** cheap money; nationalisation of the Bank.
- **1947, convertibility and the five weeks.** The pound meter.
- **Sept 1947, the plot** (Crisp): ask the leader to make way for Bevis.
- **Nov 1947, the leak:** a word to a reporter on the way into the Chamber. A dice roll on a careless remark the player chooses to make or not.
- **1948, Dunbar's death:** a private scene.
- **1949, devaluation:** Gaskell and Jagger push; Wilmot changes sides; Crisp ill in Switzerland.
- **1950–51, Gaskell's budget:** charges on teeth and spectacles. Wilmot resigns with Bowen.
