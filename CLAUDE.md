# The Member

A browser strategy game: live the whole life of a British politician of the 1938–1951 era and bend history. Read `DESIGN.md` for the agreed spec and `RESEARCH.md` for the biographical research before writing anything.

## Ground rules

- `DESIGN.md` section 1 lists decisions the project owner has already made. Do not reopen them; ask before changing any.
- When unsure, choose more detail and the more niche scenario.
- Scenario text is 60–100 words. Three or four choices each.
- Every scenario must name its real basis (which figure, which episode) and carry a "what really happened" note. Those notes are shown only in the epilogue.
- Every British politician, minister and official gets an echo name (see `DESIGN.md` section 9). Foreign leaders keep real names. Never put a real British politician's name in player-facing text outside the epilogue comparison.
- Do not invent quotations and attribute them to real people. Paraphrase.
- Facts marked (GK) or "verify" in the research must be checked against a second source before they are stated as fact in the epilogue.
- Research every figure in a camp before writing that camp. Six figures are still unread (listed at the end of `RESEARCH.md`).
- Hard subjects (Bengal famine, refugees, internment, Partition, Palestine, area bombing) are playable decisions, written soberly, with real costs.
- Dice are hidden from the player. Death is real and period-accurate.
